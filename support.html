Frequently asked questions

Does FreshFuel work offline?

The catalogue, search, and product details require an internet connection to load. Once a product screen has loaded, you can scroll through it without further connectivity. Settings such as language and region are stored on your device.

How accurate is the nutrition information?

Per-100g nutrient values are sourced from published food composition tables — including CSIRO (Australia), USDA (United States), ICBF (Colombia), and other peer-reviewed references. Actual produce can vary by variety, ripeness, and preparation. The narrative product overview shown on each detail screen is partly AI-generated and is labelled as such directly in the app.

What does the wellness lookup do?

The wellness lookup is an educational reference. You select a goal — for example, "Iron-rich foods" or "Daily energy" — and the app surfaces produce naturally rich in the relevant nutrients. It is not medical advice. For any health concern, please consult a healthcare professional.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the app and choose English or Tiếng Việt. The app defaults to your device's language on first launch.

How do I report incorrect information?

Email greenbite.dev@gmail.com with the product name, the screen where you saw it, and what you believe is incorrect. We review reports and update content where appropriate.

Does FreshFuel collect personal data?

No. FreshFuel does not require an account or sign-in and does not collect your name, email, photos, location, or health information. We collect only anonymous crash reports and App Check tokens for security. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my data?

FreshFuel does not store user accounts or personal data, so there is nothing tied to you to delete. Local app preferences (language, region) are removed when you uninstall the app.
