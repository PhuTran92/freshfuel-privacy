1. Who we are

FreshFuel is operated by Tran Hoang Phu (also known as Andy Tran), an individual sole operator, trading as GreenBite.

Email: greenbite.dev@gmail.com

2. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the FreshFuel mobile app (iOS and Android) and the GreenBite-operated backend service at api.mindfulmentor.vn handle information about you. It does not cover other websites, services, or apps that we do not operate.

3. What data we collect

FreshFuel does not require an account or sign-in, and the app does not request any device permissions (no camera, photo library, location, contacts, or microphone access).

The data we (or our service providers) collect is:

  • Crash reports — stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, and the events leading up to a crash, if one occurs (collected by Firebase Crashlytics).
  • Anonymous usage events — which screens you open, which categories you browse, which features you use (e.g. catalogue browse, wellness lookup, seasonal guide). We log feature-engagement events but never the text you type into the search bar (collected by Firebase Analytics, tied to an anonymous app-installation ID, not to your identity).
  • Performance metrics — app start-up time, screen-render time, and backend-request latency. Used to measure and improve app speed (collected by Firebase Performance Monitoring).
  • Device-attestation tokens — short-lived tokens issued by Apple's App Attest (iOS) or Google's Play Integrity (Android), sent on each backend request to verify that requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the app (collected by Firebase App Check).
  • Network metadata at the edge and server — IP address, request path, timestamp, user-agent, and TLS-handshake details, processed by Cloudflare (network edge) and our origin web server (Apache HTTP Server) for delivery, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Local on-device preferences — your selected language, last-viewed region, and similar interface state. This stays on your device and never leaves it.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any personal contact information
  • Photos, videos, or files from your device (the app has no image upload feature)
  • Your precise location
  • Health, medical, biometric, or fitness records
  • Payment information (FreshFuel has no in-app purchases or subscriptions)
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID) — FreshFuel does not request App Tracking Transparency on iOS and does not use the GAID on Android

4. How we use this data

We use the anonymous data only to:

  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and bugs
  • Understand which features people use, so we can plan improvements (e.g. which wellness goals are most viewed, which catalogue filters are most useful)
  • Measure and improve app performance (start-up time, screen-render time, backend latency)
  • Verify that backend requests come from a genuine copy of the app (anti-abuse)

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not use it for targeted advertising. We do not profile individuals.

7. AI-generated content

Some descriptive content shown in the app — particularly long-form product overviews, culinary uses, and storage tips — was generated server-side using AI (Google Gemini) and then reviewed and edited for accuracy. Your interactions with the app are never sent to Gemini. Only generic product names (such as "strawberry" or "longan") are sent during content authoring, which happens on our side, not yours. AI-generated text may occasionally contain errors; please consult authoritative sources for critical health or dietary decisions.

8. Your rights and choices

Because FreshFuel collects no personally identifying information, the available choices are:

  • Uninstall the app — the most reliable way to stop all data collection from your device. After uninstall, the app sends no further crash reports or app-attestation tokens.
  • Request information about what (if any) diagnostic data is associated with your device by emailing greenbite.dev@gmail.com.
  • Request deletion of crash data and analytics events tied to your device's anonymous installation ID by emailing greenbite.dev@gmail.com — we will pass the request to Google for processing through the Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics deletion APIs.
  • For reference: FreshFuel does not display ads, does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, does not use the IDFA (iOS) or GAID (Android), and does not perform any cross-app or cross-site tracking. Apple's and Google's device-level "analytics" / "ad personalization" settings therefore do not control any FreshFuel data flow.

9. Children's privacy

FreshFuel is a general-audience reference app. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has been collected in error, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We rely on Google's, Apple's, and Cloudflare's industry-standard security for the data described above. Communications between the app and our backend are encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we hold no user data on our own systems that could be exposed in a breach.

11. Australian users

GreenBite is committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information held about you by contacting greenbite.dev@gmail.com. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at https://www.oaic.gov.au.

12. Vietnamese users

For users in Vietnam, we follow the principles set out in Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection. You have the right to:

  • Access information about how your personal data is processed
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Data portability — receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • File a complaint with the Department of Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention and Control (A05) of the Ministry of Public Security regarding the handling of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, contact greenbite.dev@gmail.com.
